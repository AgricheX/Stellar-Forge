Custom design the parent empire of Lost Colonies (and other origins)

[h1]Instructions[/h1]
[list]
  [*] Create 1 custom empire design with a Parent Empire trait
  [*] Create 1 or more custom empire designs with the matching Lost Colony trait (eg Lost Colony 1 to match Parent Empire 1) trait
  [*] Repeat for up to 8 sets of species
  [*] Either play as your designs or set them to force spawn
  [list]
    [*] [b]IMPORTANT[/b]: make sure exactly 1 matching Parent Empire is in play (you're playing them, or you set them to force spawn)
  [/list]
  [*] Start a game!
  [list]
    [*] Each Lost Colony colony will be considered a subspecies of its matching Parent Empire regardless of traits or portrait, and are effectively a lost colony regardless of origin (homeworld is set to the parent species' homeworld, and your capital gets the Colonial Spirit modifier)
    [*] If any use the Lost Colony origin, the typical random parent will be prevented from spawning
    [*] The Lost Colony / Parent Empire traits are cleaned up (removed) from all species
  [/list]
[/list]

Optionally use this alongside [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3532904115]Paint a Galaxy[/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3762808682]Reserved Spawns[/url] to create your own map and choose where exactly where each parent / lost colony spawns!
